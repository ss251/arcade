The retained Arc testnet proof has three settled jobs: a buyer hires a seller, which hires another capability. Each child is paid from its hiring seller's working capital; the original buyer pays the root price. Hub-signed lineage binds the parent, hop and ancestors. The hub commits its recorded receipt tree, and the root settlement carries that hash on chain. A later edit would change the hash. This commits the hub's record, not a trustless account of every external action. An attempted hiring cycle was refused without a fourth payment.

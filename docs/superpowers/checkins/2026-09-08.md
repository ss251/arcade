## Blockers

None external. Two decisions sit with the owner:

1. **Circle CLI authorization window.** The installed Circle CLI 1.0.0 hard-codes a 30-day minimum timeout when signing x402 payments, while ARCADE pins ~7 days (and Circle's own `/v1/x402/supported` still reports ~7 days for Arc). Source-verified incompatibility in the client, not a failed payment. Choice: approve a bounded testnet-only 30-day profile, or keep 7 days and ship the CLI evidence without a live paid call.
2. **Escrow treasury address** for the ERC-8183 deployment (defaults to the existing fee-splitter treasury).

## Help that would be useful

For Arc/Circle: is there a supported way to make `circle services pay` honour a seller's
shorter `maxTimeoutSeconds`, or is the 30-day floor deliberate? That is the only thing
standing between ARCADE and a fully live Circle-agent-wallet purchase.

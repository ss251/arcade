## Implemented contract

requires complete context and top-level terms to agree. ENS presence is exact.
A passive export from the prior H10b1 serializer supplies the same capture rules;
approval mint/consume logic and its44 tests are unchanged. This intentionally
replaces the prior purchase-approval.ts source pin, not its historical report.

The runner burns the scope token synchronously before IO or callbacks. It retains
a captured provider method/buyer and original approved input/context privately.
It requotes before signing and forwarding; any exact context change, including
price below the ceiling, requires a new decision. It calls the actual rail-aware
signer once and direct ordinary POST once. It persists accepted H9 coordinates
before admission notification or polling, reports storage failure/recovery, and
never includes the token, signature or signed nonce in its returned view.

One five-minute monotonic lifetime includes helper limits25s/120s/10s/90s and
at most60 reads with1s between immediate pending responses. Each awaited step
also bounds an uncooperative dependency; external abort, backward/invalid clock
and late completion cannot advance subsequent IO. Timers/listeners are removed.
Observers are display-only; their exceptions do not retry payment, while an
observer-triggered abort is checked at the next action boundary. Only the actual
signing phase may produce a wallet-declined/refused distinction; downstream
errors remain unconfirmed.

Terminal output matches accepted job, original skill/amount/buyer/seller/network/
rail and actual signed nonce. Fee arithmetic, status and receipt scalar shapes
are validated. Ordinary absent settleRefKind is compatible, malformed presence
is not. EIP3009 gets a qualified hash link; Gateway gets a valid UUID without a
mined-batch claim. Unpaid output and raw errors/reasons/explorer URLs are dropped;
literal capability/signature/nonce echoes are refused. This is a closed
hub-reported projection, not independent chain verification or an XSS boundary.
Arbitrary encoded secrets cannot be detected by a literal-echo guard.

Storage records acceptance, not proven spend. Storage failure can leave current
recovery only in private RAM. Lost202 does not yield a guessed token or retry
authority. Abort cannot dismiss a remote wallet prompt, revoke an authorization,
cancel an admitted job or prove no charge. Old H9 recovery rows alone lack this
run's buyer/nonce/context and cannot claim its stronger correlation.
